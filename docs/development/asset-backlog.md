# Asset ideas backlog

This is an internal planning list, not a catalog of implemented assets. Names below are proposed identifiers and design notes only. Check the source and asset viewer before assuming an item does not already exist. Update or remove an idea when the asset is built.

A list of characters and props the library doesn't have yet, gathered by where they'd be used. None of them is a duplicate of something in `docs/library.md`. When one is made, take it off this list and give it its row there.

Each line gives the name a file would have, then what it is. A note in brackets says which game it suits, if it suits one in particular.

## Townsfolk and shoppers (ram-stealin-sam, bodega, the town games)

The crossing guard, shopper, mail carrier, old-timer and barista are made. These would fill out the street:

- `dogwalker`: five leads in one hand, each to a different dog, all going different ways. The dogs come from the quadruped builder.
- `jogger`: a headband, earbuds, a water bottle, jogging on the spot at the crossing.
- `stroller`: a parent pushing a pram, a bag of shopping hung off its handles, a toddler's shoe in the other hand.
- `busker`: an acoustic guitar, an open case with coins in it, and a cardboard sign.
- `deliveryrider`: a cycle courier's insulated cube bag on the back, a helmet, a phone strapped to the forearm.
- `cashier`: a till-roll apron, a name badge and a pricing gun. (bodega)
- `stockboy`: boxes stacked higher than his head, about to topple. (bodega)
- `lottoguy`: a fan of scratch cards, a coin, and a long hard look at each one. (bodega)
- `paramedic`: a bag and a radio. Distinct from `medic`, which is the festival tent.
- `meterwarden`: a ticket machine on a strap and a peaked cap. Everyone's favorite.
- `schoolkid`: a backpack bigger than the kid, a lunchbox and a scraped knee. Goes with the crossing guard.
- `grandma`: a tartan shopping trolley on two wheels and a rain bonnet. (Not `nonna`, who cooks.)
- `hotdogvendor`: a cart with an umbrella. `hotdog` is the mascot and `vendor` is the stadium seller, so this is a third.

## Ram-stealin-sam

- `sam`: the hero, if the game wants one in the library. A hoodie, a backpack that's too full, and a stick of RAM in each fist.
- `ramstick`: a memory module as a pickup, with its gold contacts, black chips and a label. It's a prop, glowing faintly.
- `motherboard`: a board on the floor of the level, its traces lit and its slots empty. It's scenery.
- `itguy`: a lanyard, a cable over the shoulder and a screwdriver behind the ear.
- `serverrack`: a rack with its lights blinking and a door that swings. It's scenery.

## Props, general

- `shoppingcart`: a wire trolley with a wonky wheel. It could be a hero if a game rolls it.
- `paperbag`: the old-timer's bag, as a prop of its own on the floor with groceries spilling out.
- `coffeecup`: the barista's cup as a pickup: the lid, the sleeve and a name spelled wrong.
- `stopsign`: a full road sign on its post, with the paddle's lettering. Could also be bent.
- `mailbox`: a blue post box with its slot and its collection times.
- `newspaperbox`: a coin-op paper box with the day's front page behind its window.
- `firehydrant`: a hydrant with its chain and caps.
- `trafficcone`: a cone with reflective bands. Cheap, and every level could use it.
- `bin`: a wheelie bin, its lid open and a bag sticking out.
- `cashregister`: an old till with its drawer open, keys and a bell. (bodega)
- `lottoticket`: a scratch card, half scratched. (bodega)
- `snackrack`: a wire rack of chips in the colors chips come in. (bodega)

## Dungeon

- `torchbracket`: the dungeon's torches as a prop to pick up and carry.
- `bookstack`: a pile of grimoires, one open, its pages turning.
- `skullpile`: skulls that aren't the skeleton's. They grin.
- `mimic`: a chest that is the chest, until it has teeth. It shares the chest's sculpt, with a hinge that bites.
- `cauldron`: a cauldron on three legs, the potion's color bubbling in it.

## Rock'n'roller

- `setlist`: a setlist taped to the stage, crossed out and written over.
- `mic`: a mic on a stand, the tape round the stand and the cable coiled.
- `amp`: a stack with grille cloth, knobs and the logo plate left blank.
- `drumstick`: a pair of sticks, one splintered.
- `wristband`: a festival wristband as a pickup.

## Animals

New animal models should meet the existing cat's quality bar. The list below is a set of proposals, not an active implementation plan.

- `pug`: for the dog walker.
- `seagull`: for the seafront. It shares the bird builder, and takes chips.
- `fox`: for the town at night, going through the bins.
- `squirrel`: for the park.
- `duck`: for the green's pond. It's the green's duck, made properly.
